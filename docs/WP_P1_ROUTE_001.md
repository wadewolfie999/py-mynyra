## 1. Decision and scope

### Decision

Which single bounded route, if any, now offers the best decision value toward
repeatable usable cash flow after both completed historical campaigns produced no
advancing finalist?

### Included

- inspect current public repository evidence and governing records;
- compare materially different next routes;
- identify future failure mechanisms, warning signs and failure residue;
- recommend one route, defer/reject the others and define the next readiness
  package;
- preserve all existing campaign, data, account and authorization boundaries.

### Excluded

- selecting or contacting a provider;
- creating an account, logging in, accepting terms or transmitting personal data;
- purchasing data, software, hosting, a challenge or any financial product;
- defining a new strategy, changing a prior strategy or starting a campaign;
- reading April or other sealed data;
- implementing MCP, deployment, automation or order behavior;
- using private financial values or private experiment artifacts;
- committing, publishing or merging a downstream implementation.

## 2. Baseline evidence

### Directly observed repository facts

- The canonical goal is repeatable cash actually received that materially finances
  operation while preserving survivability.
- Fifteen unique XAUUSD/M1 strategies across two completed campaigns produced no
  advancing finalist.
- Praxis Step 4 closed with all ten additional candidates rejected under its
  economic gates.
- Step 4 evaluation was not opened because independent data was unavailable.
- January–March has informed research; April and its original May tail remain
  sealed.
- Historical screening data is usable for conservative screening but does not
  provide historical bid/ask, intraminute event order or measured fills.
- The FIBO cost sample covers one hour and does not establish all-session spread,
  slippage or execution behavior.
- cTrader access is demo-only and view-only; no order path is implemented or
  authorized.
- The owner has not yet established the numeric cash, operating-coverage,
  repeatability, reserve, personal-loss, spend, debt, attempt and STOP thresholds
  required for an economic attempt or self-sufficiency claim.
- An eligible provider/account, current automation rules and a practical payout
  route have not been verified for a selected economic route.
- The coordination/MCP design is conditional on measured need and is not a
  mandatory phase of the economic path.

### Evidence limits

- Private experiment artifacts were not accessed or independently reproduced for
  this decision package.
- Provider rules and availability are time-sensitive and were not researched here;
  no provider is being recommended.
- Personal financial limits are sensitive and were not requested, inferred or
  stored in this public document.
- The comparison below is a project-route decision analysis, not evidence that any
  trading strategy or account route is viable.

## 3. Decision criteria

The selected route should:

1. remove an uncertainty that can change whether later work is worthwhile;
2. connect directly to the economic objective rather than only an enabling goal;
3. minimize irreversible time, capital, access and evidence-contamination cost;
4. leave a useful state if the route fails or returns no-go;
5. avoid dependencies that are already known to be unavailable;
6. preserve April, prior campaigns, private data and view-only broker scope;
7. have a bounded exit that does not manufacture another automatic phase.

Qualitative ratings are relative to the current state. They are not measured
probabilities or financial forecasts.

## 5. Independent future-failure analysis

### Route A — Pause or abandon

**Future failure narrative:** the project stops without a revisit condition even
though a cheap read-only feasibility check could have rejected or clarified the
economic path. External terms become stale and future sessions reconstruct the
same uncertainty.

**Earliest warning signs:** no owner disposition, no trigger/date for reconsidering
the route, and no preserved list of unresolved decision inputs.

**Failure residue:** preserved capital and evidence, but no new decision knowledge.

**Recovery:** record an explicit defer/abandon decision, reason, revisit trigger and
current preservation point.

### Route B — Economic/account/payout feasibility and risk limits

**Future failure narrative:** the package becomes an unbounded provider-shopping
exercise. Public comparisons go stale, sensitive owner information leaks into Git,
or vague targets allow a technically available account to be mislabeled viable.

**Earliest warning signs:** browsing providers before acceptance criteria exist;
missing maximum-loss/spend/attempt boundaries; comparing headline profit splits
without eligibility, drawdown, automation, payout and receipt requirements; or
placing personal values in public files.

**Failure residue:** if bounded correctly, a reusable private owner constraint
record and public provider acceptance checklist—even when the result is no-go.

**Recovery:** stop before provider screening; keep sensitive values private; mark
criteria unresolved; return to P1 without account creation, purchase or contact.

### Route C — Independent data assessment or acquisition

**Future failure narrative:** time or money is spent on data that lacks the required
quote side, coverage, provenance or execution relevance, or the data is inspected
before a candidate and evaluation contract are fixed.

**Earliest warning signs:** no exact claim the dataset must support; vendor claims
replace a sample audit; April-equivalent data is previewed during selection; or a
purchase is proposed before owner authorization.

**Failure residue:** a useful source assessment if no protected data was opened;
otherwise possible sample contamination and unrecoverable spend.

**Recovery:** restrict the first package to metadata, terms and sample-structure
assessment; require a separate acquisition and sealing decision.

### Route D — New strategy research

**Future failure narrative:** a third campaign searches variants on the same
familiar January–March history, treats parameter variants as independent ideas,
and produces another exploratory result with no admissible evaluation path.

**Earliest warning signs:** candidate rules follow observed performance; no
mechanism-level novelty; no independent evaluation source; thresholds are relaxed;
or April access is proposed to force a winner.

**Failure residue:** new code and results but increased adaptive-search burden and
less untouched evidence.

**Recovery:** stop before result inspection; preserve the proposal; require a new
registered protocol and independent-evaluation decision.

### Route E — Coordination or MCP work

**Future failure narrative:** the project builds a state service because the design
exists, without proving that coordination is the limiting problem. Maintenance,
hosting and client integration consume time while the economic route remains
undefined.

**Earliest warning signs:** architecture or deployment begins before the operator
handoff is measured; no handling-time baseline; no actual client requirement; or
the service is treated as authority rather than a source-bound view.

**Failure residue:** documentation or a local prototype may be reusable, but it
does not advance BG-01 and can create an ongoing maintenance obligation.

**Recovery:** stop at the measurement result; select no MCP package unless its
expected saved effort exceeds build, review, maintenance and recovery cost.

## 6. Recommendation

### Recommended G1 disposition

Advance **Route B — resolve economic/account/payout feasibility and owner risk
limits** to an owner decision for a bounded P2 readiness package.

This route is preferred because:

- it addresses conditions that must be satisfied before spending or economic
  exposure regardless of which strategy is eventually selected;
- it can reject an unusable economic path before more strategy, data or
  infrastructure investment;
- its first package can remain read-only, private where necessary and free of
  account/provider interaction;
- a no-go result leaves reusable acceptance criteria and prevents avoidable cost;
- it neither opens April nor depends on a current strategy finalist;
- it preserves Routes C, D and E as later options when a concrete decision need
  justifies them.

### Disposition of other routes

- **Route A:** retain as the fallback if the owner does not want to define the
  constraints or if no acceptable economic route is plausible.
- **Route C:** defer until a selected research/evaluation claim defines the required
  independent data and Route B shows that the economic path merits the investment.
- **Route D:** defer until a materially new hypothesis, admissible evaluation path
  and registered contract exist.
- **Route E:** defer as the main route; allow a separate measurement-only package
  if coordination demonstrably blocks Route B or a later selected route.

The owner subsequently approved this recommendation and authorized opening

## 7. Proposed downstream package

### `WP-P2-ECON-001` — Establish economic-route acceptance criteria

**Decision enabled:** is there at least one account/provider/cash-receipt route
worth investigating under the owner's actual economic and survivability limits?

**Bounded preparation sequence:**

1. **Private owner constraint contract:** establish the minimum net cash target and
   period, required operating coverage, repeatability duration, reserve, maximum
   personal loss/spend/debt, attempt count and STOP conditions. Store sensitive
   values only in an explicitly authorized private location.
2. **Public acceptance schema:** translate the private constraints into non-sensitive
   pass/fail categories for eligibility, automation, drawdown, leverage, fees,
   payout, jurisdiction, cash receipt, maintenance and failure residue.
3. **Read-only assessment plan:** define source hierarchy, freshness, contradiction
   handling, evidence capture, candidate limit and time bound before researching
   current providers.
4. **Readiness decision:** approve a bounded provider screen, return unresolved
   inputs, or close no-go/defer without contacting or purchasing from anyone.

**P2 exit evidence:**

- owner-accepted private constraint record or explicit unresolved fields;
- public non-sensitive provider acceptance checklist;
- exact data sources and freshness rules for later current-provider research;
- provider-screen candidate/time limit;
- STOP conditions and no-go rules;
- explicit authority for the read-only screen, if selected.

**Prohibited in this package:** provider contact, account creation, login, identity
submission, terms acceptance, purchase, payment, credentials, broker scope change,
orders, strategy research, sealed-data reads and public disclosure of personal
financial values.
